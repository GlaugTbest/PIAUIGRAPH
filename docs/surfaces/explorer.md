# Explorer

Mode: Operate. User arrives to find a municipality, understand data coverage, and follow a procurement item to its supplier and evidence.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A precise geographic investigation workspace. The municipality selection is the hinge connecting map, list, comparison and network.

OWN-WORLD: User-pinned wine/red palette and Times-like Tinos serif. Compact labelled controls, paper-light surfaces, thin separators and dark text. Red communicates selection, never guilt.

STORY: Begin in Piauí, search a municipality, choose an official procedure, read comparable item results, inspect a supplier's connections, open the source.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wine masthead with name and methodology navigation; compact territory/period/search toolbar; restrained statewide coverage strip; large municipal map at left and a fixed contextual panel at right. Signature move: selecting territory draws its boundary while the matching evidence list updates with the same scope. Mobile stacks the map and collapsible panel without hiding search.

FORM: geographic workspace pinned by the approved brief; seed: none (user-pinned direction). Motion is limited to map movement and short, reduced-motion-aware state changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
