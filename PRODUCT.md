# PIAUIGRAPH

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js, TypeScript, MapLibre GL JS, Neo4j. Vercel Hobby. User authorized implementation, commits, push, public deployment and statewide expansion after the three-municipality pilot passes on 2026-10-29.

## Users
People exploring public procurement in Piauí, including journalists, researchers and citizens. Personal, non-commercial portfolio shared on LinkedIn and TikTok.

## Product Purpose
Explore official records by territory, compare equivalent medicine items and inspect evidence-backed supplier relationships. The application describes observations, never concludes fraud.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Initial years 2024 and 2025; monthly refresh; R$0/month services; anonymous public reading.
- All 224 official municipalities searchable; 19 IBGE immediate regions.
- Pilot: Pimenteiras, Gilbués, Milton Brandão; statewide expansion gated on functional and analytical tests.
- Official, traceable data only. Missing fields are unknown, not zero. Publication year and procedure year remain separate.
- PNCP procurement procedures, item results and signed contracts are distinct entities and financial stages.
- Neo4j must be real and exercised, including Cypher and provenance, from the MVP. Public projections may be cached for performance; never claim a live database when serving a snapshot.
- Do not infer personal identities, beneficiary territory, fraud, payments or actual spending from award values.

## Brand Commitments
Portuguese interface. Serif typography resembling Times. Palette #73030D, #732F3B, #F2F2F2, #8C8C8C, #0D0D0D. Map first, no marketing landing page. Desktop contextual side panel, mobile bottom panel, equivalent list/table. Impeccable quality workflow.

## Product Principles
1. Every factual record and graph edge links to an official source.
2. Coverage is reported, never implied by the presence of a map.
3. Fast navigation uses bounded, versioned, demand-loaded projections.
4. Failed ingestion never replaces a valid release.
5. A small or unmatched sample produces an honest insufficient-data state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled controls, adequate contrast, semantic tables and alternative list access to map/graph. Reduced motion supported.
